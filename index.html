Common commercial leaks

Where Businesses Usually Lose Time & Opportunities

These problems rarely look dramatic on their own. Together, they create delays, missed revenue and too little management visibility.

  • New enquiries are handled differently depending on who sees them first.
  • Quotes and prospects are not followed up consistently.
  • Customer information sits across inboxes, spreadsheets, CRM notes and people’s heads.
  • Managers cannot easily see what is stuck, overdue or worth acting on.
  • Teams spend too much time chasing, copying, checking and repeating admin.
  • Useful market or competitor information exists, but nobody has time to track it properly.
